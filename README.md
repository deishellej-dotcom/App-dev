# Romeo and Juliet
## About the Story
*Romeo and Juliet* is a famous tragedy written by William Shakespeare. It is about two young people who fall in love despite their families being enemies.

> A story about love, conflict, and the importance of peace.

## Main Characters
 **Romeo Montague** – A young man who falls in love with Juliet.
 **Juliet Capulet** – A young woman who loves Romeo.
**Mercutio** – Romeo's close friend.
**Tybalt** – Juliet's cousin.
**Friar Laurence** – A priest who helps Romeo and Juliet.

## My Favorite Part
My favorite part is when Romeo and Juliet express their love for each other despite their families' disagreement. It shows how love and understanding can exist even during difficult situations.

## Lessons I Learned
1. We should not judge people because of their family or background.
2. Hatred and conflict can cause serious problems.
3. We should think carefully before making decisions.
4. Peace and understanding are better than fighting.

## Why I Like It
I like *Romeo and Juliet* because it is an interesting and meaningful story. It teaches lessons about love, loyalty, family, and the consequences of conflict.

**Author:** William Shakespeare

**Genre:** Tragedy and Romance

**Themes:** Love, Family, Conflict, and Fate
